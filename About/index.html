Who We Are

Dandwat Pranam

I’m Kausik, 26 years old and the founder of the 108 Dharma Yatra. My spiritual journey began about four years ago when I started following Krishna consciousness. Since then, I have been practicing spiritual disciplines and spent a significant amount of time in a temple, where I studied Vedic scriptures and learned about the Vedic way of life.

During this time, I developed a deep connection and understanding of Lord Narasimha Dev, which inspired me to share this knowledge with others. That’s why I started the 108 Dharma Yatra channel and this website—to spread awareness about Lord Narasimha Dev and to highlight the importance of temples in our lives.

My goal is simple: to share what I’ve learned and support others on their spiritual journey.
